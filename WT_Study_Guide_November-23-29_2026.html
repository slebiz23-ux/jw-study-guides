4. How did the priests of Israel represent God before man?
▸ Show comment▾ Hide comment

The first of two ways priests helped worshippers was by serving as teachers, representing God before the people. Malachi 2:7 says:

“For the lips of a priest should safeguard knowledge, and people should seek the law from his mouth, because he is the messenger of Jehovah of armies.”

6. In patriarchal times, what role did a family head have that was similar to that of a priest?
▸ Show comment▾ Hide comment

Jehovah has always wanted a peaceful relationship with his obedient worshippers. Long before Israel became a nation, the family head offered sacrifices for himself and his family:

  • Noah offered sacrifices of thanksgiving after leaving the ark. Gen. 8:20
  • Abraham built altars to sacrifice to Jehovah. Gen. 12:7, 8
  • Job offered burnt sacrifices on behalf of his children.

That is why the Bible speaks of priests even before the Levitical priesthood existed. Jehovah said:

“And let the priests who regularly come near to Jehovah sanctify themselves, so that Jehovah may not strike them.””
Ex. 19:22
The article explains that these "priests" were the family heads.
