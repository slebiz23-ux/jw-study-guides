❓ How Would You Answer?
How can a single Christian choose a mate who will be a good lifelong friend?

By relying on Jehovah’s guidance through his Word: (1) choosing a fellow worshipper — only marrying “in the Lord” (1 Cor. 7:39); (2) asking the right questions during courtship about character, spiritual depth, and goals (Col. 3:9, 10); (3) seeking the observations of mature Christians who know the person well; and (4) not ignoring honest doubts. A good mate is “from Jehovah.” (Prov. 19:14)

What can help a married couple to strengthen their friendship?

Making intentional time for each other — scheduling it like any important commitment — doing activities together including things the other is passionate about (Matt. 7:12), minimising distractions, and especially spending time together spiritually: Bible reading, prayer, and field service. As “iron sharpens iron,” two Christians together grow sharper. (Prov. 27:17)

What can help a married couple to stick together when challenges arise in their marriage?

Not being quick to separate, but instead reflecting on how to apply the love qualities of 1 Corinthians 13:4-8a: “Love is patient and kind. Love is not jealous. It does not brag, does not get puffed up, does not behave indecently, does not look for its own interests, does not become provoked. It does not keep account of the injury. It does not rejoice over unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth. It bears all things, believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things. Love never fails.” (1 Cor. 13:4-8a) Seeking Jehovah’s help through prayer, using the organization’s publications, and consulting elders. Keeping Jehovah as the third strand of the cord ensures the marriage won’t “quickly be torn apart.” (Eccl. 4:12)
