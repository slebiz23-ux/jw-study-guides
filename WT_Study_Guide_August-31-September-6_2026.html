Why we need to keep listening attentively during meetings and other spiritual programs and how we can do so.

Getting Started

PARAGRAPHS 1-2
(a) How does Jehovah care for the spiritual needs of his people today? (b) What will we consider regarding Christian meetings?
Psalm 23:1, 2, 5 2 Peter 1:12

Suggested Answer

(a) Jehovah cares for our spiritual needs today by providing "an abundance of spiritual food," much as a shepherd cares for his sheep. David captured this beautifully: "Jehovah is my Shepherd. I will lack nothing." (b) In this article we will consider why we need to listen carefully at meetings, how those who teach can help others to listen, and practical suggestions that can help us become better listeners — even when the material is familiar. As Peter reminds us, we need reminders "although" we already "know" the truth and are "well-established" in it.

Use Psalm 23 with someone who feels spiritually adrift or unsupported — it shows a personal, caring God who actively provides for his people, not a distant force.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah

This shows Jehovah as an attentive Shepherd who anticipates our needs and reminds us of things we already know, because he understands we need reinforcement to stay spiritually strong.

1TODAY, Jehovah is providing us with an abundance of spiritual food. We can certainly agree with David, who sang: "Jehovah is my Shepherd. I will lack nothing."

2One way in which Jehovah provides spiritual food today is through our congregation meetings. At these meetings, we often review information that is familiar to us. Even if we "are well-established in the truth," we need these reminders. However, when spiritual programs include material that has been covered many times, do you find it hard to pay attention? This article can help you. We will consider why we need to listen carefully at meetings. We will also see how those who teach in the congregation can help others to listen. And we will discuss some suggestions that can help us become better listeners. But first, let us examine who is speaking to us.

Remember That Jehovah Is Speaking to Us

PARAGRAPH 3
How does Jehovah speak to us, and how do his servants make good use of his Word?
2 Tim. 3:16, 17 John 7:15 Acts 17:2, 3 Acts 18:24, 28 Luke 24:32

Suggested Answer

Jehovah speaks to us through his Word, the Bible — all Scripture is "inspired of God and beneficial for teaching." Jesus used the Scriptures so skillfully that listeners were amazed he had such knowledge without formal schooling. Paul and Apollos likewise reasoned from the Scriptures with others. Our talks, broadcasts, and videos are all Bible-based, and speakers should make God's Word the centerpiece of their teaching, not just reading it but showing how to apply it — just as the disciples on the road to Emmaus felt their "hearts burning" as the Scriptures were opened up to them.

💬 Comment you could share

📌 Ministry connection

When someone asks "why should I trust what you say," point them straight to the Scriptures being read and explained, not just your own words — this mirrors how Paul and Apollos reasoned "from the Scriptures."

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah

Jehovah chose to communicate with us through his written Word rather than leaving us to guess — he wants a real, ongoing conversation with his people.

3Jehovah speaks to us by means of his Word, the Bible. Jesus understood this. He used God's Word so skillfully that his listeners were moved to ask: "How does this man have such a knowledge of the Scriptures when he has not studied at the schools?" Honesthearted people were thrilled when they heard Jesus explain God's Word. First-century Christians, such as the apostle Paul and Apollos, also made liberal use of the Scriptures when they reasoned with others. We, as Jehovah's Witnesses, know that the Bible is the basis for our articles, broadcasts, talk outlines, and videos. Many visitors are impressed by the way Jehovah's people make use of the Bible at their meetings. Those who have parts at such meetings make God's Word the centerpiece of their teaching — not only by reading from it but also by showing the audience how to apply what it says.

Therefore, when Jehovah speaks to us by means of his Word at our meetings and we come to understand what his will is, it is easier to obey him. We can trust that Jehovah is pleased when we listen to him and act accordingly.

Why Should We Listen Carefully at Meetings?

What does our love for Jehovah move us to do?
Matt. 22:37 John 4:23, 24 1 John 5:3

Suggested Answer

We listen at meetings because we love Jehovah. The more we learn about him, the more our love grows, and that love stirs in us the desire to listen, obey, and worship him the way he has set out in his Word — with spirit and truth, not through burdensome commandments.

💬 Comment you could share

"I found it interesting that love for Jehovah is described as growing the more we learn about him — so paying attention at meetings isn't a duty, it's actually a way of building that love."

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah

Jehovah's commandments "are not burdensome" — he doesn't ask for empty ritual but for worship that flows from genuine love, "with spirit and truth."

5We listen at meetings because we love Jehovah. The more we learn about Jehovah, the more our love for him grows. That love stirs in us the desire to listen to him, to obey him, and to worship him in the way he has set out in his Word.

At meetings Jehovah teaches us how to help fellow believers and those who don't yet know him — for example, through the brochure Love People—Make Disciples, which develops qualities that benefit our ministry, including how to engage people in friendly conversation.

If so, pay special attention to the way your brothers and sisters demonstrate that skill in the midweek meeting student parts.

How to Help Others to Listen at Meetings

PARAGRAPH 8
Why should brothers who teach from the platform continue to improve in their speaking ability?
2 Tim. 4:2 Titus 1:9

Suggested Answer

A well-prepared, Bible-based talk has tremendous potential to draw people to the truth and help believers stay in it. Those with speaking parts shouldn't underestimate the good they can do — the following points, while aimed at public talks, apply in principle to any speaking assignment.

8Brothers who teach from the platform, especially those who give public talks, should continue to improve in their speaking ability. A well-prepared, Bible-based presentation has tremendous potential to draw people to the truth and to help fellow believers stay in the truth. Accordingly, those who have parts at the meeting should not underestimate the good they can do. If you have that privilege, consider how you might apply the following points in your talks. Although these points are directed especially to those who deliver public talks, they can be applied in principle to any speaking assignment we might receive.

PARAGRAPH 9
Why is it important for a talk to be easy to follow?

Suggested Answer

Prepare a clear structure. The audience may become distracted if material is too complicated. Steps: (1) make the main points clear at the outset, (2) cover points one at a time with clear transitions, (3) briefly review the points in conclusion.

📌 Ministry connection

The same "clear structure" principle helps in an informal witness — state your main point up front rather than rambling, so the householder can follow easily.

9Prepare a clear structure. As a speaker, you want your material to be easy to follow. The audience may become distracted if the material is too complicated. What can you do to give a well-structured talk? Here are some steps: (1) Try to make it clear at the outset what your main points will be. (2) Cover these points one at a time, pausing between main points and clearly stating the next main point before moving on. (3) Briefly review the points in your conclusion.

PARAGRAPH 10
Why is it important to get excited about the material?
Prov. 2:4, 5 Acts 4:20

Suggested Answer

You'll speak with enthusiasm if you develop points you personally love. Meditating on the scriptures and assigned material helps you find these points — much as one who "searches" for wisdom "as for hidden treasures" comes to understand and value it deeply.

💬 Comment you could share

"The experience of the brother who still remembers, decades later, a talk that taught him to meditate deeply shows how much impact genuine enthusiasm for the material can have on a listener."

10Get excited about the material. You will speak with enthusiasm if you develop points you love. Meditating on the scriptures and the assigned material will help you find these points. A brother from the United States who is in his 70's recalls a talk he heard when he was in his teens: "I still remember how the speaker encouraged us to meditate deeply when reading God's Word. He spoke with conviction and explained clearly how to do it. That talk marked a turning point in my life; it affected my Bible reading as well as my relationship with Jehovah."

PARAGRAPH 11
Why is it helpful to give practical, specific suggestions in talks?
1 Tim. 4:13-16

Suggested Answer

Think about what your audience needs, and offer practical suggestions rather than general information. Paul gave Timothy specific, actionable counsel to stay spiritually strong; a good speaker does the same for listeners.

11Think about what your audience needs, and offer practical suggestions. Rather than present general information, use the Scriptures to give specific encouragement and suggestions. For example, the apostle Paul gave Timothy a number of specific suggestions to help him stay spiritually strong in his assignment in Ephesus. Think about what your listeners need, and help them to see practical ways that they can apply the information in their life.

PARAGRAPH 12
Why is it best to speak naturally when giving a talk?

Suggested Answer

Practicing out loud lets you speak conversationally rather than reading word for word — as the brochure Apply Yourself to Reading and Teaching notes, a natural, sincere delivery conveys how you feel about the topic and your listeners, making it more lively and motivating.

12Practice your talk out loud so that you can speak in a conversational manner. When you speak naturally instead of writing out your talk word for word and reading it, those in the audience will find it easier to listen and absorb the information. The brochure Apply Yourself to Reading and Teaching says: "Speak in a natural, sincere way that conveys how you feel about the topic and your listeners." As you speak from the heart, your audience will find your delivery more lively, interesting, and motivating.

PARAGRAPH 13
Why should teachers in the congregation ask experienced speakers for counsel?
Prov. 1:5

Suggested Answer

Share your notes beforehand and ask an experienced speaker to listen to your delivery for suggestions. We all have "blind spots" and may not see whether we're making points clearly — a wise person "listens and takes in more instruction."

💬 Comment you could share

"What struck me is the phrase 'blind spots' — even skilled speakers can't fully evaluate themselves, so asking for counsel isn't a sign of weakness, it's wisdom."

13Ask an experienced speaker for counsel. How might you do this? You may want to share your notes with an experienced speaker beforehand and thereafter include his suggestions in your talk. Additionally, ask him to listen to your delivery with a view to giving you suggestions on how you might improve as a teacher. We all have "blind spots," and we may not be able to discern if we are making our points in a clear and helpful way. We want to improve as speakers and teachers so that we can help our listeners benefit from the spiritual gems found in God's Word.

Ask an experienced speaker to help you improve as a teacher (paragraph 13)
Application: If you have a speaking assignment this week, consider sharing your outline with a mature brother beforehand — his outside perspective can catch things you can't see in your own preparation.

How to Become a Better Listener

PARAGRAPH 14
Why should we cultivate the attitude of the writer of Psalm 119?
2 Tim. 3:1 Ps. 119:24, 111, 167

Suggested Answer

We become better listeners by deepening our appreciation for Jehovah's reminders. We need God's reminders to cope with "critical times hard to deal with." When material feels repeated, we do well to meditate on the psalmist's attitude toward God's reminders — treating them as advisers, a permanent possession, and something to love dearly.

14We will become better listeners by deepening our appreciation for Jehovah's reminders. We need God's reminders in order to cope with challenges that are common during these "critical times hard to deal with." If at times we feel that we have heard the same information many times before, we do well to meditate on the attitude the writer of Psalm 119 had toward God's reminders.

PARAGRAPH 15
How can we maintain our appreciation for the spiritual food we receive at meetings?
Ex. 16:15, 31 Num. 21:5 Prov. 3:13, 16-18 John 17:3

Suggested Answer

We must maintain a positive view of spiritual food, just as the Israelites should have kept a positive view of manna — it came from Jehovah, tasted good, and sustained life, yet some grew tired of it. We must never view spiritual food as commonplace, but recognize Jehovah as the Source, remember it's thoughtfully prepared, and reflect that the wisdom gained can preserve our life — indeed, coming to know Jehovah and Jesus Christ IS everlasting life.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah

Jehovah personally and thoughtfully prepares our spiritual food through his organization — it's never "commonplace," even when a topic is familiar.

15We need to maintain a positive view of the spiritual food we receive, just as the Israelites should have maintained a positive view of Jehovah's provision of the manna when they were in the wilderness. No doubt the Israelites at first appreciated the manna. It came from Jehovah, it tasted good, and it sustained their life. In time, however, some grew tired of eating manna every day. The lesson? We must never view the spiritual food we receive as commonplace or needlessly repetitive. But instead, we need to recognize that Jehovah is the Source. We must remember that it is thoughtfully prepared by Jehovah's organization. We should also reflect on the reality that the wisdom we gain from it can preserve our life.

PARAGRAPH 16
What has helped some longtime servants of Jehovah to keep listening attentively at meetings?

Suggested Answer

We become better listeners by applying practical suggestions. Ashley (30+ years serving) says preparation is key. József (52 years) enjoys familiar topics because different angles deepen understanding, and focuses on the message rather than a speaker's style if he isn't outstanding.

16We can become better listeners by applying practical suggestions. Notice what has helped some longtime servants of Jehovah to keep listening attentively at meetings. Ashley, who has been serving Jehovah for over 30 years, says: "Preparation is key to paying attention. If I am not prepared, I find it hard to answer and my mind wanders." József, who has served Jehovah for 52 years, says: "I enjoy listening to familiar topics because viewing the material from different perspectives deepens my understanding." And he adds: "If a brother is not an outstanding speaker, I focus on the message he conveys from God's Word instead of his speaking style."

PARAGRAPH 17
What can you do to listen attentively at meetings?

Suggested Answer

Ten practical suggestions: (1) eat lightly beforehand, (2) arrive early and find your seat, (3) silence electronic devices, (4) anticipate what will be covered, (5) stay focused on the speaker, (6) take brief notes or simple drawings, (7) think about the key scriptures and logical order, (8) ask "how can I use this to help someone?", (9) at assemblies preview the day's theme and key scriptures, (10) prepare your heart by sitting quietly before the program starts.

💬 Comment you could share

"I found the suggestion about silencing electronic devices and not allowing them to distract us especially practical — it's such a simple, concrete step."

17Is it possible that applying one or more of the following suggestions might help you listen attentively? (1) If you eat before the meeting, be sure to have a light meal so that you can remain alert. (2) Arrive early and find your seat before the program starts. (3) Set electronic devices on silent mode and do not allow them to distract you during the meeting. (4) Anticipate what will be covered. (5) Stay focused on the speaker. (6) Take brief notes, or make simple drawings of key points. (7) Try to think about the key scriptures and the logical order in which they are presented. (8) Ask yourself, 'How can I use this to help someone in the congregation or in the territory?' (9) At assemblies and conventions, preview the day's theme, the subjects to be presented, and the key scriptures for each talk. (10) Prepare your heart by sitting quietly and paying attention to the music and video presentations that are played at the start of the program.

Keep listening to God's reminders, not allowing electronic devices to distract you (paragraph 17)
Application: Consider silencing your phone completely (not just on vibrate) before this Sunday's Watchtower Study, as a concrete way to apply suggestion (3) above.

Keep Listening to Jehovah

PARAGRAPH 18
Why should we keep listening at meetings?
Ps. 23:3 31:3 Luke 8:18 Ps. 119:2, 14

Suggested Answer

By listening at meetings, we allow Jehovah to lead us in "the paths of righteousness." We need corrective counsel so we don't drift off course like a wind-driven boat. So we should "pay attention to how" we listen and apply the guidance received — those who listen to Jehovah will be happy now and have everlasting life in the future.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah

Jehovah actively leads and guides us like a shepherd steering a course — his corrective counsel is a form of care, not criticism.

18We should keep listening at meetings because by doing so, we allow Jehovah to lead us in "the paths of righteousness." We need the corrective counsel we receive so that we will not drift off course like a boat driven off course by the wind. Therefore, "pay attention to how you listen" and apply the guidance you receive at congregation meetings. Those who listen to Jehovah will be happy now and have everlasting life in the future.
