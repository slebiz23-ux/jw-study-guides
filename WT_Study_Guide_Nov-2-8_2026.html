Introduction — Why We Can Trust in Jehovah (¶1-3)

1. What earthshaking events are ahead of us? (¶1-2)

The nations will give their power to the United Nations, which will then turn on false religion — the start of the great tribulation. Afterward the nations will attack Jehovah's people, leading to Armageddon, when Jesus and his heavenly forces remove this world.

Scriptures: Rev. 17:16 Rev. 17:17 Ezek. 38:14-16 Rev. 16:14-16 Rev. 19:11-21

A Psalm About Trust and Protection (¶4-6)

3. How does the opening of Psalm 91 assure us of protection? (¶4)

Psalm 91 opens by assuring us Jehovah protects those who trust him. Words like 'refuge' and 'stronghold' recall walled cities, and the psalm uses vivid pictures — such as a bird sheltering its young — to show he can guard us from any danger.

Scriptures: Ps. 91:1 Ps. 91:2 Ruth 2:12 Ps. 17:8 Ps. 91:3

💬 Comment you could share: The imagery of a mother bird covering her young made Jehovah's care feel personal and tender to me.

📌 Ministry connection: I can use the 'refuge' picture when talking to someone who feels unsafe or alone.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah protects with both strength (a stronghold) and tenderness (wings).

4. How does the end of the psalm build our confidence? (¶5-6)

In the closing verses Jehovah himself speaks, using 'I will.' His promises are certain because he never fails to do what he says. Jehovah had Psalm 91 recorded for our benefit, and we can personally echo its trust by calling him our refuge.

Scriptures: Ps. 91:14 Josh. 23:14 Num. 23:19 Rom. 15:4 Ps. 91:2

💬 Comment you could share: What stood out is the shift to Jehovah speaking directly — his own word is the guarantee.

📌 Ministry connection: When a Bible student doubts promises, I can highlight that Jehovah always fulfills his word.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah is faithful and cannot lie.

What Kind of Protection Does Psalm 91 Promise? (¶7-10)

5. What kind of protection does Psalm 91 promise? (¶7)

The psalm primarily promises spiritual security, and it also points to protection from physical and emotional harm for God's people as a group.

Scriptures: Ps. 91:7

📌 Ministry connection: Helps me explain to others that Jehovah's protection is real but not magical.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah cares about our whole person — body, heart, and friendship with him.

6. What can we learn from Jesus' response to Satan? (¶8-9)

Satan quoted Psalm 91 to tempt Jesus into a reckless stunt. Jesus did not deny the psalm promises physical protection — he refused to test Jehovah. Angels have protected God's people before, but not those who act recklessly.

Scriptures: Matt. 4:5-7 Ps. 91:11 2 Ki. 6:15-17 2 Ki. 19:35 Dan. 3:26-28 Dan. 6:16-22

💬 Comment you could share: It was sobering that Satan can twist Scripture — I want to know God's Word well enough to spot misuse.

📌 Ministry connection: A practical angle: trusting Jehovah never means taking foolish risks.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah protects the faithful, yet expects us to use good judgment.

How Does Jehovah Protect Us Now? (¶11-17)

8. How does Jehovah protect us spiritually? (¶11)

Jehovah helps us fight sin and the spiritual dangers that could harm our relationship with him. He fully understands how strongly sin pulls on imperfect humans.

Scriptures: Rom. 6:16 Rom. 7:21-23

💬 Comment you could share: I'm comforted that Jehovah understands my struggles instead of being harsh about them.

📌 Ministry connection: Many people feel trapped by bad habits — Jehovah's help is a real hope to share.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah is understanding and compassionate toward our imperfection.

9. How can we win the fight against sinful desires? (¶12-13)

God's Word warns against wrong desires and shows how to fight them early — Jesus taught that the sin starts in the heart. Continued exposure to immoral images stirs desire, so we must cut them out of our lives completely.

Scriptures: Matt. 5:28 Matt. 5:29 Jas. 1:14 Jas. 1:15 Col. 3:5

💬 Comment you could share: The key thought for me: stop sinful thoughts before they grow.

📌 Ministry connection: Shows the Bible's practical wisdom for modern problems like pornography.

🙏 Getting to know Jehovah: Jehovah's laws are protective, like guardrails.

Jehovah sometimes allows individuals to suffer persecution or death, which helps prove Satan a liar. But Satan will never wipe out God's people as a group — angels protect them so pure worship continues.

13. Why shouldn't we expect protection from all physical harm now? (¶17)

Living by Jehovah's principles guards us from many dangers, but if he shielded everyone from all harm, Satan could claim we serve God only for what we get. Jehovah is patient with the wicked for his name's sake, and he helps us endure trials now.

Scriptures: Prov. 14:30 Prov. 22:3 2 Cor. 7:1 Job 1:10 Rom. 9:22 1 Cor. 10:13 1 Pet. 5:10

How Will Jehovah Protect Us in the Future? (¶18-20)

14. How will Jehovah protect us during the great tribulation? (¶18)

He will keep protecting his people spiritually. When the nations attack, he will not let his worshippers be wiped out as a group, leading to Armageddon, where Christ and his armies act against God's enemies.

Scriptures: Ezek. 38:10 Ezek. 38:14 Rev. 17:14 Rev. 19:11 Rev. 19:14

📌 Ministry connection: Share that the Bible foretells a victory for good.

15. Why need we not fear Armageddon? (¶19-20)

God's enemies will be destroyed, and none of his sheep will be killed by human or demonic enemies. Psalm 91's promises may literally come true then — protection physically, emotionally, and spiritually, as a group and as individuals.

Scriptures: Jer. 25:33 Zech. 14:12 Luke 21:28 2 Pet. 2:9 Ps. 91:7
